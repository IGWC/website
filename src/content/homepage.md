# Indiana Graduate Workers Coalition (IGWC) is a member-run labor union representing over 1,700 graduate workers and students at Indiana University, Bloomington.

<section class="split">

<div class="third vertical">

Since 2019, we've fought for ([and won](/victories)) significantly better pay, better working conditions/benefits, and the end of exorbitant graduate student fees—all in a state without friendly labor laws. 

We're able to win in hostile conditions because of our commitment to **member-driven collective action**, and that means we need you!

# What Are We Fighting For?

<section>

<div class="red">

## Our Degrees

<div class="twothird">

### Protection of At-Risk Degree Programs

IU must take measures to protect degree programs and associated departments at risk of dissolution under HEA-1001-2025 through negotiations with elected bargaining representatives for the graduate workers, students, faculty, and staff impacted.

### Secured Funding

IU must provide living wage employment for graduate students to finish their degree, including alternative sources for programs for which federal funding has been cut, without financial burden placed on departments or faculty grants.

</div>
</div>

<div class="red">

## Our Work

<div class="twothird">

### Union Recognition

IU must recognize the IGWC as the collective bargaining representative for graduate workers.

### Living Wage

IU must provide a path to universal 12-month contracts for SAAs with a living wage for all graduate workers, which according to the MIT cost of living standard is currently $43k/year.

</div>
</div>

<div class="red">

## Our Campus

<div class="twothird">

### Legal Protection

In addition to maintaining enrollment, IU must ensure that international students receive financial support to finish their degrees regardless of their student or visa status

### Sanctuary Campus

IU must refuse to cooperate with federal immigration enforcement and must provide the fullest protection to students of vulnerable populations.

</div>
</div>

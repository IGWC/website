# Votes of No Confidence in Whitten’s Administration

On Monday, February 5, 2024, the Indiana Graduate Workers Coalition passed [a vote of no confidence](/archives/No-Confidence-Resolution_Feb-4-GMM.pdf) in IU President Pamela Whitten's Administration. The vote of no confidence comes as a result of President Whitten's failure to respond to [an IGWC demand, signed by 1,300 graduate employees, for a union election and a living wage](/archives/Recognition-Demand-Letter_Jan-2024-t6gl.pdf), continuing to ignore the majority of graduate workers' attempts to initiate a bargaining relationship with the IU Administration.

The vote of no confidence also cites the recent [suspension of Professor Abdulkader Sinno](https://www.idsnews.com/article/2024/01/faculty-iu-violated-university-policy-abdulkader-sino-suspension) and the [cancellation of IU alumna Samia Halaby](https://www.idsnews.com/article/2024/01/palestine-art-iu-samia-halaby-exhibition-cancel)['](https://www.idsnews.com/article/2024/01/palestine-art-iu-samia-halaby-exhibition-cancel)[s exhibition](https://www.idsnews.com/article/2024/01/palestine-art-iu-samia-halaby-exhibition-cancel) as evidence that the Whitten administration is not only acting against the best interests of the university but is also suppressing fundamental rights.

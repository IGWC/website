date: 2022-09-27
listserv: igwc-ue-news-l
---

In light of ongoing negotiations with the Bloomington Faculty Council and University Graduate School, the [IGWC-UE Coordinating Committee recommended voting “no” on strike authorization, and “yes” to allow the Coordinating Committee to set a later strike date](/news/cc-statement-no-vote-strike-sep-2022), should the IU Administration fail to offer the union suitable recognition and shared governance. [General Membership agreed](https://twitter.com/IndianaGrads/status/1573338707171721216), in a vote that demonstrates graduate employees’ willingness to engage in good-faith negotiations with the administration, and urges the Administration to do the same.

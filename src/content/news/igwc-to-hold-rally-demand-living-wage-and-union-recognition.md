---
title: Indiana Graduate Workers Coalition To Hold Rally, Demand Living Wage And Union Recognition
description: <##>
date: 2023-09-25
---

The Indiana Graduate Workers Coalition (IGWC) is holding a rally for “More Say, More Pay” on Thursday, September 28, where IGWC will launch a major campaign to encourage graduate workers to sign union cards in support of a living wage and union recognition. The event will take place from 12:00 p.m. until 1:00 p.m. at the Sample Gates. [Read full press release here](/archives/More-Say-More-Pay-Rally_9252023.pdf).
